# Figure Legends

**Figure 1. Cross-cohort definition and bulk validation of a grade-associated meningioma aggressiveness program.**  
(a) A 200-gene aggressiveness program was defined from primary tumours in GSE136661 using the WHO grade I versus II contrast and projected into the independent GSE16581 cohort. The WHO I/II separation in GSE136661 is therefore in-sample; WHO III tumours provide held-out extrapolation. (b) In GSE74385, recurrence was analysed separately from malignant progression. Recurrence-only versus non-recurrent tumours yielded AUC 0.822 and remained associated after simultaneous adjustment for grade and batch (OR per 1 SD=5.18, 95% CI 1.40–25.83, p=0.0118); malignant progression and the batch-confounded grade-I view are reported only in Supplementary Figure S1/Table S1. (c) Gene-set enrichment analysis identified oxidative-phosphorylation and ribosome-biogenesis enrichment with lower synaptic/Wnt programs. (d) A 56-gene LASSO classifier achieved mean repeated nested-cross-validation AUC 0.86 and external AUC 0.82 for grade and 0.83 for recurrence; these are retrospective discrimination estimates, not a deployable threshold.

**Figure 2. Single-cell architecture of GSE183655 and compartment-level projection of the aggressiveness program.**  
(a) The GSE183655 meningioma atlas contained 63,628 QC cells and resolved patient-specific tumour/tumour-like mesenchymal cells together with myeloid, T-cell, endothelial and mural compartments. Putative meningioma tumour/tumour-like cells were assigned as non-immune, non-endothelial and non-mural patient-specific mesenchymal cells; because CNV confirmation was not performed, the manuscript uses tumour/tumour-like terminology. (b) The bulk-derived aggressiveness score was broadly distributed and is shown descriptively rather than interpreted as cell-type-specific enrichment. (c) Patient-of-origin patterns are shown as an annotation check. CellChat analyses are presented only as descriptive co-expression-based candidate interactions.

**Figure 3. Grade-stratified single-cell validation in GSE206647.**  
(a) The independent grade-stratified GSE206647 cohort contained 163,897 QC cells from 16 grade-labelled tumour patients plus 2 normal meninges and resolved 71,717 tumour/tumour-like meningioma cells together with immune and vascular compartments. (b) Cell-level AddModuleScore distributions are shown descriptively and were not used for inference. (c) To avoid cell-level pseudoreplication, tumour/tumour-like cells were aggregated per patient into summed-count pseudobulk profiles before scoring the 200-gene program. The per-patient pseudobulk score rose with WHO grade (Spearman rho=0.87, p=1.2×10⁻⁵; ANOVA p=7.5×10⁻⁵), with a clear WHO I to II increase and saturation between WHO II and III. A cell-level mixed model with patient random intercept retained the grade association after covariate adjustment. In a separate patient-balanced sensitivity analysis, the grade association persisted in CopyKAT-aneuploid pseudobulks from 15 eligible patients (rho=0.868, p=2.71×10⁻⁵; Table S6); CopyKAT is transcriptome-based CNV inference, not DNA confirmation.

**Figure 4. Cross-modal prioritisation of candidate aggressiveness markers.**  
(a) Transparent internal-evidence scores integrate discovery rank, patient-level GSE206647 tumour/tumour-like pseudobulk grade association, tumour-cell expression specificity and directional recovery in GSE16581/GSE74385. Scores are ranking aids rather than inferential statistics. (b) Among the 159 measurable program genes, 56 showed directionally aligned patient-level grade associations at BH-FDR<0.10. *PI3* and *PITX1* passed the corrected patient-level threshold; *NF2* and *LTK* are labelled to show, transparently, that neither passed that threshold. (c) Exact evidence fields for these four prespecified genes are displayed without combining them into a druggability claim. These results nominate *PI3* and *PITX1* for orthogonal tissue validation, not functional dependencies or validated therapeutic targets.

**Figure 5. Secondary patient-level Geneformer representation benchmark.**
(a,b) Geneformer V2-104M was applied in zero-shot mode to 1,800 GSE206647 tumour/tumour-like cells from 16 patients. Visualisation is cell-level and descriptive; all inference keeps patients intact. (c) In the primary patient-mean-embedding PCA, the best top-10 component was PC2 (rho with grade=−0.684; rho with patient program score=−0.665), with a 100,000-iteration patient-label permutation p=0.0516. A complementary cell-PCA-then-patient-mean analysis yielded PC4 (rho with grade=−0.856; rho with program score=−0.688; p=0.00070), showing sensitivity to aggregation order. (d) Repeated nested leave-one-patient-out grade discrimination was high for both Geneformer embeddings (mean AUC 0.977) and program-gene expression (0.992), but their paired difference was uncertain (classical minus Geneformer −0.016; patient-bootstrap 95% CI −0.094 to 0.000). With 16 patients these estimates are descriptive, and no cell-weighted AUC is reported.
